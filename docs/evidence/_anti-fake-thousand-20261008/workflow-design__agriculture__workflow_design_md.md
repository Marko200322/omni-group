## Process step 1: Discover local leads

Action: discover
Primary module: client-hunter
Owner role: Growth / ops lead
SLA: respond or advance stage within 4 business hours

## Automations, KPIs & rollout checklist

Automations to enable after manual dry-run:
• Discover local leads: auto-discover with human override
• Score leads: auto-score with human override
• CRM capture: auto-create-contact with human override
• Outreach sequence: auto-sequence with human override
• Follow-up: auto-run with human override
• Billing: auto-invoice with human override

Agriculture KPI set:
• Yield vs plan
• Lot traceability completeness
• Input waste %

Operational risks to watch:
• Missed harvest windows
• Input inventory stockouts
• Traceability gaps on lots

Rollout checklist:
• ☐ Day 1: map stages in CRM
